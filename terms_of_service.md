2. **Daily Rate Limit & Duplicate Lock:** Each member may send a maximum of **10 connection requests per rolling 24-hour window** (enforced server-side from database records). You cannot send a new request to the same mentor while a prior request is `pending` (returns HTTP `409 CONFLICT`).
3. **14-Day Auto-Expiry and 7-Day Cooldown:** If a mentor does not accept or decline a connection request within **14 days** of creation (`expires_at = created_at + 14d`), the request automatically expires and the sender is notified. If neither party has enabled any shareable contact fields, the Platform flags this in the response and prompts both members to update their privacy settings.

### 7.3 Private Member Blocking (`MENT-06`)
You may block any member at any time (`POST /blocks`) and manage your block list in **Settings → Blocked Members** (`GET /blocks`, `DELETE /blocks/:userId`).
- Blocking is **immediate and confidential** (no notification is sent to the blocked member).
- Once blocked, neither party can view the other's full profile (both see a generic *"Profile Unavailable"* state), neither party appears in the other's search results, and the blocked member is barred from sending connection requests to you.
