## 1. Welcome to OASIS ONE: One Community. Infinite Possibilities.

OASIS ONE is the trusted digital home for Odisha's startup community—bringing together 650+ aspiring entrepreneurs, startup founders, co-founders, mentors, and advisors. Unlike open social networks or unmoderated chat groups, every member of OASIS ONE is vetted and approved.

These Community Guidelines and Content Policy govern how we interact, share knowledge, celebrate milestones, seek mentorship, and discuss funding opportunities. Every member must accept this document upon registration and re-accept updated versions when published.

## 2. Core Principles of Participation

### 2.1 Be Generous, Specific, and Search-First
- **Search Before Posting:** Before publishing a new question, use the global search bar (`⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`) to check if a similar question has already been answered.
- **Write Clear, Useful Posts:** When creating a post (`POST /posts`), select the right category (**Question**, **Suggestion**, **Resource**, or **Opportunity**) and tag it with accurate sectors (*DeepTech, AI/ML, FinTech, HealthTech, AgriTech, EdTech, ClimateTech, SaaS, D2C/E-commerce, Hardware/Manufacturing*) and functional areas (*Funding, Sales, Marketing, Product, Technology, Hiring, Partnerships, Legal/Compliance, Operations*).
- **Respect Character Limits & Edit Windows:** Post titles are capped at **200 characters**, post bodies at **10,000 characters**, and responses at **5,000 characters**. You may edit your own post within **24 hours** of publication (which adds an `"edited"` badge and preserves the original text for moderation records).
- **Close the Loop (`POST-08`):** When a community member or mentor answers your question, mark the post as **Resolved** and highlight the accepted answer so future entrepreneurs searching the portal can immediately find the solution.

### 2.2 Celebrate Real Milestones in the Achievement Banner
- The dashboard **Achievement Banner** (`POST /achievements`) highlights verified entrepreneurial progress—such as launching an MVP, securing a patent or grant, signing a pilot customer, completing an incubator cohort, or making a key technical breakthrough.
- Keep achievement submissions concise (**max 500 characters** and an optional JPEG/PNG image **max 5 MB**).
- Every achievement is reviewed by an administrator before going live (`pending_review`). Once approved, it shines on the community dashboard for **24 hours (UTC)** before expiring automatically.
- **Do not** submit generic sales flyers, discount codes, festival greetings, or unverified claims as achievements.

## 3. Prohibited Content and Conduct (`POST-03` & PRD §21.2)

To keep OASIS ONE safe, high-signal, and compliant with Indian law (including Rule 3(1)(b) of the *Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021*), the following content and behaviors are strictly prohibited and subject to immediate reporting (`POST /reports`) and moderation:

### 3.1 Harassment, Bullying, and Doxxing (`reasonCategory: harassment`)
- Personal insults, abusive language, targeted harassment, intimidation, or stalking of any member, mentor, applicant, or administrator.
- Hate speech or discrimination based on caste, religion, region, language, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, or disability.
- **Doxxing & Privacy Breaches:** Publishing or screenshotting another member's private phone number, private email address, residential address, or confidential connection messages without their explicit permission.

### 3.2 Spam, Cold-Pitching, and Directory Harvesting (`reasonCategory: spam`)
- Posting repetitive promotional advertisements, service-agency sales pitches, affiliate links, or mass promotional messages across community threads.
- Misusing the member directory (`/profiles`) or mentor discovery (`/mentors`) to harvest leads, build external marketing lists, or scrape member data.
- Adding any OASIS member or mentor to external WhatsApp groups, email newsletters, or CRM pipelines without their prior opt-in consent.

### 3.3 Misinformation, Impersonation, and False Credentials (`reasonCategory: misinformation`)
- Knowingly publishing false or misleading information regarding government startup schemes, Startup Odisha / DPIIT policies, grant deadlines, or ecosystem regulations.
- Impersonating another entrepreneur, mentor, investor, corporate executive, or government official.
- Fabricating academic credentials, corporate titles, startup traction, or mentor verification evidence (`ProfileChangeRequest`).

### 3.4 Unlawful, Infringing, or Confidential Material (`reasonCategory: other`)
- Sharing content that infringes copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, or patents, or that breaches a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA).
- Uploading files containing malware, spyware, macros, or disguised executables.
- Publishing defamatory, obscene, pornographic, gambling-related, or otherwise unlawful material under the laws of India.

## 4. Fraudulent Funding Claims & Investor Solicitation Policy (Mandatory Policy — PRD §21.2)

Because entrepreneurs frequently seek capital, grants, and co-founders on OASIS ONE, we enforce a strict **Zero-Tolerance Policy on Financial Fraud and Improper Solicitation**:

### 4.1 Prohibition on Fraudulent Fundraising & Guaranteed Returns
Members must **never** post, promote, or solicit participation in:
- Ponzi schemes, pyramid schemes, multi-level marketing (MLM) programs, or chit funds;
- Unregistered collective investment schemes, unregulated token/crypto offerings, or "guaranteed return" investment schemes;
- False claims of having secured venture capital, angel investment, or government grants when no such funding has been awarded.

### 4.2 Strict Ban on "Pay-to-Pitch" and Advance-Fee Scams
- No member, mentor, advisor, or external partner may demand **upfront fees, consultation charges, "processing fees," or commission retainers** from founders as a condition for reviewing pitch decks, introducing investors, or facilitating grant applications on OASIS ONE.
- Mentorship on OASIS ONE is community-driven. Any mentor or member found soliciting advance fees for funding introductions will face **immediate Account Revocation and Permanent Blacklisting**.

### 4.3 Rules for Sharing Funding Calls, Grants, and Opportunities
When sharing a funding opportunity, incubator call, hackathon, or grant under the **Opportunity** or **Resource** post category:
1. **Include Verifiable Official Sources:** Always include the official `https://` website link of the granting body, incubator, or institutional fund.
2. **State Clear Eligibility & Deadlines:** Clearly specify who is eligible to apply, the application deadline, and whether you are affiliated with the organizing entity.
3. **No Public General Solicitation of Securities:** Founders must not use public community posts to conduct an unlawful public offering of shares or securities in violation of the **Companies Act, 2013** or **SEBI regulations**.

### 4.4 Member Due Diligence Disclaimer
While OASIS vets member identities at onboarding, **OASIS does not independently audit startup financial statements, verify every member-posted opportunity, or endorse any investment or co-founding arrangement.** Members are solely responsible for conducting independent legal, financial, and background due diligence before signing agreements, sharing proprietary IP, or transferring funds.

## 5. Mentor Discovery & Connection Etiquette (`MENT-01` to `MENT-06`)

### 5.1 How to Request Mentorship Respectfully
- **Check Fit First:** Before clicking **Request Guidance / Connect** on a mentor's profile, verify that their listed sectors, expertise tags, and preferred startup stages match your needs, and check whether they hold a **Verified Mentor** badge.
- **Write a Clear, Actionable Request:** State briefly who you are, what stage your startup or idea is at, and the specific question or challenge you would like their perspective on.
- **Respect Platform Quotas & Cooldowns:**
  - You may send at most **10 connection requests per rolling 24-hour period**.
  - You cannot send a duplicate request while one is already `pending`.
  - Mentors have **14 days** to respond before a request auto-expires, and a **7-day cooldown** applies after a declined or expired request. Do not attempt to bypass a declined or expired request by harassing the mentor on external channels.

### 5.2 Standards for Mentors and Advisors
- **Manage Your Availability:** If your schedule is full, switch your mentor status to **Paused** in **Settings → Profile** (`PATCH /me/profile`) so members know you are temporarily unavailable.
- **Respect Founder Confidentiality:** Treat unpublished business ideas, technical architectures, and pitch decks shared by mentees with strict professional discretion.
- **Disclose Conflicts of Interest:** If you advise, invest in, or operate a competing startup in the same niche as a mentee, disclose that conflict upfront before accepting sensitive information.

### 5.3 Protecting Shared Contact Information
When a connection request is accepted (`POST /connections/:id/accept`), the Platform shares only the contact details each party has enabled under `shareOnConnection`. You must treat shared phone numbers and email addresses as **strictly confidential 1-on-1 contact details**—never share them with third parties or add them to bulk mailing lists.

## 6. Event & Meetup Conduct (`EVT-01` to `EVT-10`)

1. **Commit to Your RSVP or Cancel Early:** Physical meetups in Bhubaneswar and across Odisha have limited seating. If your plans change after registering, cancel your registration on the event page (`DELETE /events/:id/register`) as early as possible so the next member on the waitlist is automatically promoted.Repeated no-shows at capacity-limited physical events may result in event registration restrictions.
2. **Keep Online Meeting Links Confidential:** Online meeting URLs (`meeting_link`) are unlocked only for confirmed registered attendees. Never share or repost an OASIS event meeting link publicly or in external groups.
3. **Professional Conduct at Meetups:** All harassment, anti-spam, and no-solicitation rules in these Guidelines apply equally at physical OASIS meetups, workshops, and demo days.

## 7. Profile Integrity and Flagged Profile Changes (`PROF-02`)

To prevent "bait-and-switch" identity changes after approval, any edit you make to your **Primary Role, Full Name, Startup Name, Expertise Areas, or Mentor Verification Evidence** is placed in a `ProfileChangeRequest` queue for administrator review. Your existing approved details remain displayed until an administrator verifies and approves the update.

## 8. Content Moderation, Enforcement Ladder, and Appeals

### 8.1 Reporting Violations (`POST /reports`)
If you encounter a post, response, achievement, or profile that violates these Guidelines:
1. Click **Report** on the item and select the most accurate category (`spam`, `harassment`, `misinformation`, or `other`) along with optional details.
2. Reports are routed confidentially to the Admin Moderation Queue (`GET /admin/reports`). Your identity as the reporter is never disclosed to the reported member.
3. Once a moderator resolves the report, you will receive an in-app resolution notice (`NOT-06`).

### 8.2 Content Removal (`POST-04`)
Moderators and Super Admins may hide or remove any post, response, or achievement that violates these Guidelines. Every moderation action requires a documented reason, is permanently recorded in the `AuditLog`, and sends the author a notification with a generic reason explanation.

### 8.3 Graduated Account Enforcement Ladder (PRD §10.2)
Depending on the severity of a violation and whether a member has prior infractions, administrators enforce the following graduated actions:
1. **Warning (`warning`):** A formal written warning attached to your account record for minor or first-time guideline breaches.
2. **Temporary Suspension (`suspension`):** Temporary loss of Platform access until a set expiration date (`expires_at`). While suspended, your existing posts remain visible but are locked against new replies, and your event registrations are held.
3. **Account Revocation (`revocation`):** Permanent termination of membership for serious or repeated violations. Your login is disabled, your posts and responses are soft-hidden, and all shared connection contact info is immediately revoked.
4. **Permanent Blacklist (`blacklist`):** Issued by a Super Admin for severe violations (such as financial fraud, doxxing, harassment, or malicious scraping). Permanently bans you and blocks future re-registration using hashed email and phone identifiers.

### 8.4 How to Appeal a Moderation or Restriction Decision
We believe in fair, accountable moderation. If you believe your post/achievement was hidden in error or that an account warning, suspension, revocation, or blacklist was applied unfairly:
1. Submit an appeal within **15 days** of the notification via the in-app **Contact OASIS** form (`/contact` — accessible even while suspended) or by emailing **Info@oasisodisha.in**.
2. Include your registered email address, the notification date, and a clear explanation of why the action should be reconsidered.
3. If your appeal involves alleged bias or misconduct by an administrator or moderator, select the **Admin Misconduct (`admin_misconduct`)** category on the support form, which routes your appeal directly to the independent OASIS Oversight inbox (`OVERSIGHT_EMAIL`).
4. Appeals are reviewed within **15 days**, and the outcome is logged in the Platform's `AuditLog`.
